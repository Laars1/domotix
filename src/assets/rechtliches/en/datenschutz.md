## 3. Web analytics with Rybbit

We use Rybbit for the statistical analysis of how our website is used. We operate Rybbit on a server in Switzerland.

Rybbit does not use analytics or advertising cookies and does not store any such information on your device.

The data collected includes in particular the pages visited, time spent on the site, referrer, browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and an approximate location derived from the IP address.

The IP address is used to determine the approximate location and is not stored permanently. To distinguish between visits, a pseudonymous identifier is generated from technical information. The analytics data is used exclusively for internal statistical purposes.
