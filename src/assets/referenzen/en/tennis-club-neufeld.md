## Starting point

The tennis courts already had KNX-based building automation. The court lighting was additionally to be linked to the GotCourts booking system in order to reduce energy consumption and avoid unnecessary light emissions for the surrounding area.

## Solution

Loxone was integrated as an intelligent interface between GotCourts and the existing KNX installation. Current bookings are evaluated via the GotCourts API and passed on to the building automation. Combined with the ambient brightness, the court lighting is thus controlled automatically and on demand via KNX. The lights are only on when they are actually needed.
