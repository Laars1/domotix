## Starting point

A high-end single-family home was to be equipped with modern building automation featuring a consistent and intuitive control concept. Lighting, shading, room climate, audio and other building functions were to be easy to operate and centrally networked.

## Solution

The building automation is based on KNX and combines the various building services into a centrally controllable overall system. The DALI lighting is integrated, as are shading, room climate control and other building functions. Individually configured JUNG KNX push-buttons provide intuitive operation, while a Gira X1 handles central visualisation; door communication was likewise realised with an integrated Gira solution.
