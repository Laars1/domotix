## Starting point

An existing golf simulator was to be comprehensively modernised, both technically and structurally. The low ceiling height posed a particular challenge: the projector had to be positioned outside the tee area, while the ceiling also had to be protected against accidental strikes from golf clubs.

## Solution

The golf simulator's technical infrastructure was comprehensively modernised and adapted to the room. A new custom-made screen was integrated and the projector positioned to suit the tee area. In addition, the ceiling was fitted with protective cladding, allowing the technology to blend into the room neatly and discreetly.
