## Starting point

An apartment building was to receive comprehensive building automation that combines modern living comfort with classic, high-quality interior architecture. A particular challenge was to integrate the modern technology into the design concept as unobtrusively as possible while still allowing intuitive operation.

## Solution

The entire building automation was planned and implemented with Loxone. Lighting, heating, shading and access were brought together in one central system. Conventional porcelain switches were integrated into the Loxone control and complemented by custom-made temperature sensors designed to match the sockets. A special detail is the custom-made brass doorbell with integrated Loxone technology, which blends harmoniously into the appearance of the building.
